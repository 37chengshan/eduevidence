# EduEvidence — Judge Evidence Pack

> Decision-grade evidence engine for education and social-science questions.
> Every claim below points to a machine-checkable artifact in this repository.

## The five cards

1. **Problem Card** — why a normal Deep Research skill cannot take responsibility for a decision.
2. **Method Card** — counter-evidence, methodology audit, evidence boundary, decision loop.
3. **Architecture Card** — Workflow → Capability DAG → Contract; adapters and projections are orthogonal.
4. **Benchmark Card** — same-model controlled comparison isolating method from model.
5. **Real-world Card** — real-literature flagship, Policy portability, and the honest boundary between implemented and pending evidence.
