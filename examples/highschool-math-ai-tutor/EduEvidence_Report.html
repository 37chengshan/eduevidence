Disagreement among studies is explained by three critical boundary conditions: (1) Feedback Granularity: In-task speed gains (+0.68g) occur when AI provides step-level hints, whereas giving complete answers causes severe delayed retention loss (-0.31g) due to cognitive offloading. (3) Teacher-in-the-Loop Mediation: Rural school deployment with teacher dashboards narrows equity gaps (+0.35g), while unassisted home access shows null effect (+0.05g to +0.12g).

06 Evaluation Plan & Sources Appendix

Difference-in-Differences evaluation design and complete peer-reviewed sources

Research question: In national curriculum high school mathematics, does a 4-phase fading adaptive AI tutor intervention enhance students' conceptual modeling and unassisted exam performance without inducing scaffolding dependency, and does teacher mediation narrow urban-rural achievement disparities?

Baseline: Week 1-2 standardized pre-test covering prerequisite junior high algebra, plane geometry, and diagnostic coordinate transformation items.

Post test: Week 16 standardized unassisted closed-book final examination covering analytic geometry and calculus applications (identical paper across groups).

Retention: Delayed unassisted closed-book retention exam administered 4 weeks post-intervention to measure long-term schema retention.

Transfer: Non-routine mathematical modeling transfer tasks administered during the final unassisted assessment.

Process metrics

  • In-task problem completion speed and step-level derivation accuracy
  • Weekly AI interaction frequency and hint request patterns
  • Hint exploitation and rapid-click gaming rate from platform logs
  • Teacher dashboard review fidelity and remedial intervention frequency

Learning metrics

  • Standardized unassisted final examination score (primary outcome)
  • Delayed 4-week unassisted retention exam score
  • Geometric modeling and multi-representation transfer task subscore
  • Baseline-to-final standardized learning gain (ANCOVA effect size)

Risk metrics

  • Unassisted solo task completion rate without hint availability
  • Illusion of competence index (self-assessed confidence vs. unassisted score gap)
  • Urban-rural achievement gap trajectory across baseline and post-tests

Success threshold: Treatment group unassisted final exam score is statistically non-inferior to control (margin d > -0.15), achieves significant gain in geometric modeling transfer (d >= +0.30, p < 0.05), exhibits no delayed retention deficit, and narrows the urban-rural gap by at least 0.20g under teacher mediation.

Analysis plan: Multi-site cluster randomized controlled trial (24 high schools: 12 urban, 12 county/rural) combined with Difference-in-Differences (DID) estimation. Primary hypothesis evaluated via ANCOVA with baseline pre-test as covariate and school cluster random effects. Subgroup heterogeneity analyzed by school tier (urban key vs. county/rural) and prior achievement tertiles.
