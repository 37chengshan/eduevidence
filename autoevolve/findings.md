# Autoevolve Findings

Record reusable lessons from completed experiments, including failures and near-misses. `results.tsv` and `experiments.jsonl` remain the machine-readable source of truth.

Template:

```text
## EXP-...
Hypothesis:
Observed result:
Why it likely happened:
What this rules out:
What should be tried next:
Complexity/cost lesson:
```

A failed experiment is still a finding when it rules out a plausible path.
